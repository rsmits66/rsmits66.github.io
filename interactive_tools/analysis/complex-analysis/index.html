The Riemann sphere

Stereographic projection gives every point of the extended complex plane, the point at infinity included, a place on the unit sphere x12 + x22 + x32 = 1.

On the sphere

Drag a marked point to move it. Drag anywhere else to turn the sphere.

In the z-plane

One line through the north pole

Join a point P of the sphere to the north pole N = (0, 0, 1) by a straight line. The line meets the equatorial plane in exactly one complex number z. The southern hemisphere lands inside the unit circle, the northern hemisphere outside it, and N itself is left to stand for ∞.

Circles go to circles, or to lines

A plane α1x1 + α2x2 + α3x3 = α0 with unit normal α and 0 ≤ α0 < 1 cuts the sphere in a circle. Its image in the plane is a circle too, unless the circle on the sphere runs through N. Then α0 = α3 and the image is a straight line. This is why every straight line is said to pass through ∞.

Distance measured on the sphere

Take the straight chord between the sphere points of z and z′. Its length d(z, z′) never exceeds 2, the diameter, and it treats ∞ like any other point. Far from the origin, numbers that are a long way apart in the plane sit close together near N.
